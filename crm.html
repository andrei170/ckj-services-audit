Timings are a starting point. Tenders on the larger schemes run far longer than a roofing quote, so 30, 60 and 90 days is probably closer than 7 or 14 — and it can differ by value, so the small ones get chased sooner and the big ones are left alone.
Where the enquiries come from
CKJ's site carries no enquiry form — everything arrives by phone or to kris@. So the system is built around that rather than around web forms.
