Timings are a starting point. A tender on a large scheme runs far longer than a roofing quote, so 30, 60 and 90 days is likely closer than 7 or 14 — and it can vary by value, so the small ones get chased sooner and the big ones are left alone.

On site, on a phone

Most of this is not something you sit at a desk for. The point of the phone view is that a quote can be updated from a gate on a Tuesday morning rather than from a notebook three days later.

Where enquiries actually come from
CKJ's website carries no enquiry form — everything arrives by phone or to kris@. So this is built around that, not around web forms.
Email to kris@
Becomes a record automatically
Phone call
Logged against the contractor with a note
ITT / tender pack
Attached to the quote record
Repeat contractor
Matched to their existing history
Website enquiry
If a form is ever added, it lands here too
Nothing here asks you to change how you work. The enquiries arrive exactly as they do now. The difference is that they stop living only in your head and your inbox.
