The Finding That Matters Most

Your website says you are audited. The Scaffolding Association says you are assessed.

Your About page states you are "audited members of the Scaffolding Association". The Association's own public directory lists CKJ Services Limited as "Assessed", and you do not appear on their Audited Contractors list.

Those are two different tiers with two different prices. Assessed is a form and a fee. Audited adds an on-site audit, and it is the tier built to align with the public procurement standard buyers now screen against.

This matters more than a wording slip. The people you want to win work from check exactly this, and finding the claim overstated is worse than not making it. It is also the most concrete, fixable thing in this entire audit — the gap between where you are and where you say you are is one audit booking away.

The Thing Worth Being Honest About

Advertising will not win you big commercial projects. That is not how the work is let.

Main contractors do not find scaffolders through search. Kier requires verification to Constructionline Gold or the Common Assessment Standard, and states plainly that holding it gives no automatic right to enquiries. Morgan Sindall requires a supplier to be sponsored by one of its regional business units before it is even invited to complete a prequalification questionnaire.

Frameworks are closed and long. One regional scaffolding framework we looked at appoints up to sixteen suppliers across four years. Another appoints three per lot.

So "three or four more big projects a week" is not a target this market can produce, and anyone selling you Google Ads to achieve it is selling you something that will not work. The honest version is that commercial growth comes from accreditation, prequalification and relationships — and the website's job is to survive the check that happens after somebody puts your name forward.

Where advertising does work for you is the other half of your business: local domestic work, and trade-to-trade capture from roofers, renderers and builders who need a scaffolder and search for one. That is a real, winnable lane, and your old site was already pointed at it.
